STRICT IMPLEMENTATION RULES

SOURCE OF TRUTH
1. Brand Identity PDF controls logo, brand colours and typography direction.
2. Master prompt + FINAL_IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.txt control website implementation.
3. Page TXT files control page-specific content/structure.
4. 01_ARCHITECTURE_STRATEGY_REFERENCE_ONLY.pdf is strategy reference only; do not copy its blue/neon presentation palette into the website.

COLOUR — DO NOT CHANGE
Use these exact values:
- Ivory #F7F3EA
- Green #214D33
- Gold #C49A3A
- Red #8F2D24

Do not introduce blue, neon yellow, purple, gradients, or another palette.

TYPOGRAPHY
Use:
- Outfit (brand guide label: "Outfit (Garet Alf)") as primary
- The Seasons as secondary/editorial

Do not substitute Garet Sans as the primary website font.
Do not implement the brand guide's -60% letter-spacing literally. Start near -0.03em for H1 and tune visually.

DESIGN
- Premium editorial hospitality
- Warm ivory + deep green foundation
- Gold/red restrained
- Large natural photography
- Generous whitespace
- Fine lines
- Asymmetry used carefully
- Minimal cards
- No glassmorphism
- No loud glow effects
- No neon presentation-deck styling

ANIMATION
Animations are encouraged, but they must be calm, cinematic and meaningful.
Preferred:
- Arunachala hero reveal
- Slow depth/parallax
- Healing journey scroll sequence
- Living typography
- Farm-to-table transition
- Interactive Journey selection
- Property walkthrough
- Horizontal experience narrative
- Day-at-Gold-Mountain timeline
- Subtle CTA hover/magnetic effects

Use GSAP/ScrollTrigger only where it adds real value. Prefer transform/opacity animation. Honor prefers-reduced-motion and simplify complex motion on mobile.

CONTENT SAFETY
Do not invent rooms, prices, practitioners, certifications, awards, facilities, testimonials, medical claims, availability, package durations or founder achievements.
Use [CONTENT TO BE PROVIDED BY CLIENT] and [PRICE TO BE PROVIDED] where needed.

BOOKING
This is enquiry-first, not live booking:
- Primary: WhatsApp
- Secondary: Email
- Optional verified phone

Never create fake calendars, inventory, payments or booking confirmations.

PRODUCT TERMS
Keep these distinct:
Short Stay = flexible accommodation
Wellness Stay = accommodation + flexible wellness experiences
Monthly Stay = longer accommodation
Wellness Package = structured confirmed programme

ACCESSIBILITY / PERFORMANCE
Build toward WCAG 2.2 AA quality:
- keyboard navigation
- visible focus
- semantic HTML
- proper labels
- useful form errors
- target sizes >= 24x24 CSS px where applicable
- adequate text contrast
- reduced motion
- no focus hidden by sticky UI

Use WOFF2, font-display: swap, responsive images, dimensions/aspect-ratio reservations, optimized AVIF/WebP, and lightweight animation.

Do not improvise the design direction.
Do not add sections simply to fill space.
Do not redesign the logo.
Do not change the content strategy.
Do not turn the site into a generic booking engine or Ayurveda clinic.
