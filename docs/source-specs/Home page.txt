BUILD PAGE: HOME

PURPOSE:
The homepage is the primary storytelling page. Its job is to create desire, communicate positioning and guide the visitor toward Wellness, Stay, Experience and Enquiry.

CANONICAL INFORMATION HIERARCHY:
1. HERO
2. WELLNESS
3. LOCATION / ARUNACHALA
4. DINING / NOURISHMENT
5. RESORT / PLACE
6. EXPERIENCE
7. STAY
8. TRUST
9. BLOG
10. BOOK / ENQUIRE

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 01 — HERO
--------------------------------------------------
Use a full-width immersive image of the actual resort/nature/mountain surroundings.

Headline direction:
A Wellness Stay
in the Presence of Arunachala.

Supporting copy:
A peaceful resort where traditional wellness, healthy food and nature come together.

Primary CTA:
Explore Wellness

Secondary CTA:
Plan Your Stay

Do not overcrowd the hero.
Use the approved logo in the navigation.

Animation:
Slow image reveal + subtle depth movement + restrained headline/CTA reveal. No loud effects. Keep initial content visible quickly.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 02 — WELLNESS
--------------------------------------------------
This is the strongest section after the hero.

Headline:
Wellness, in its own time.

Include only client-confirmed offerings. Candidate themes:
- Ayurveda
- Traditional therapies
- Yoga
- Meditation
- Healthy food
- Nature
- Lifestyle

Avoid clinical/guaranteed health claims.

CTA:
Explore Wellness

Animation:
A calm scroll-led BODY → MIND → FOOD → LIFESTYLE → ENVIRONMENT sequence may be used.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 03 — LOCATION / ARUNACHALA
--------------------------------------------------
Headline direction:
In the presence of Arunachala.

Explain only confirmed geography and relationship to Tiruvannamalai.
Do not exaggerate views or distance.

Use a map/location visual where appropriate.

Animation:
Slow landscape depth/mist/foreground movement; reduced-motion fallback is static imagery.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 04 — DINING / NOURISHMENT
--------------------------------------------------
Headline:
Food that nourishes.

Position food as part of the wellness experience without unsupported medical claims.

Use actual food photography.

CTA:
Discover Dining

Animation:
Farm → Garden → Kitchen → Table visual progression where real assets support it.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 05 — THE RESORT
--------------------------------------------------
Introduce Gold Mountain as a resort first.

Headline direction:
Stay close to nature.
Stay close to yourself.

Show only real/approved property imagery:
- Architecture
- Rooms
- Gardens
- Common spaces
- Natural surroundings

CTA:
Explore the Resort

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 06 — EXPERIENCE
--------------------------------------------------
Introduce unique experiences only after client confirmation.

Potential areas:
- Shiva Shakthi Darshanam
- Cow shelter
- Fish pond
- Five-element philosophy
- Farm-grown vegetables
- Herbal garden

Headline:
More than a stay.

Supporting idea:
A place shaped by nature, tradition and a deeper connection to the surroundings.

CTA:
Explore the Experience

Animation:
Editorial horizontal or guided-scroll storytelling; do not turn every item into an animated card.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 07 — STAY
--------------------------------------------------
Show confirmed accommodation categories only.

Each item:
- Image
- Room name
- Short description
- Key amenities if confirmed
- Enquire / View Stay

Do not show fake prices or availability.

Use the taxonomy defined in the Master prompt. Do not mix Wellness Packages with room categories.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 08 — TRUST
--------------------------------------------------
Use genuine information only:
- Confirmed testimonials
- Confirmed guest feedback
- Founder/practitioners where client-approved
- Genuine ratings if verified
- Real photography

Never fabricate testimonials, statistics or credentials.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 09 — BLOG
--------------------------------------------------
Use BLOG instead of GALLERY.

Show up to 3 featured articles.
Use the canonical categories:
- Wellness
- Ayurveda
- Food
- Nature
- Arunachala
- Life at Gold Mountain

SEO topics such as Tiruvannamalai travel can be used as article topics/tags without forcing a second category taxonomy.

Do not create fake articles. Use clearly marked placeholders until real articles exist.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 10 — FINAL CTA
--------------------------------------------------
Headline:
Your time at Gold Mountain begins here.

Supporting text:
Plan your stay, explore our wellness offerings or simply speak with us.

Primary CTA:
WhatsApp Us

Secondary CTA:
Send an Enquiry

Email must remain clearly available.
