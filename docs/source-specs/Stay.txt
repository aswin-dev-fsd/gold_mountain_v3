ROOMS
--------------------------------------------------

Photography

CTA:

--------------------------------------------------
STAY TYPES
--------------------------------------------------
Keep these distinct:

SHORT STAY
Flexible accommodation for guests who want a simple stay and freedom to explore.

WELLNESS STAY
Accommodation combined with confirmed wellness experiences.

MONTHLY STAY
Longer accommodation option; details only when confirmed.

WELLNESS PACKAGE
Structured programme with confirmed duration, inclusions and price. Do not confuse this with a room/stay type.

--------------------------------------------------
