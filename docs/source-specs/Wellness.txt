BUILD PAGE: WELLNESS

PURPOSE:
Introduce Gold Mountain's wellness philosophy without making the property look like an Ayurveda clinic.

The visitor should understand:
Wellness here is an experience.

It can include confirmed traditional practices, nourishing food, movement, meditation, nature and lifestyle.

--------------------------------------------------
HERO
--------------------------------------------------
Headline:
A slower way back to yourself.

Supporting copy:
Explore traditional wellness practices, nourishing food and quiet spaces designed to help you reconnect with body and mind.

Avoid wording that promises a medical outcome.

CTA:
Explore Our Wellness

Animation:
Slow photographic reveal + typography entrance. Keep motion subtle.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 02 — WELLNESS PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------
Explain:
Gold Mountain brings together traditional wellness practices and the restorative qualities of nature.

Keep copy short.
Do not make medical claims.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 03 — WELLNESS APPROACH
--------------------------------------------------
Create a clean editorial composition containing confirmed topics such as:
Ayurveda
Traditional therapies
Yoga
Meditation
Healthy food
Nature
Lifestyle

Use minimal line icons only where they clarify meaning.
Do not make this look like a generic icon-card grid.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 04 — AYURVEDA
--------------------------------------------------
Introduce Ayurveda as one important part of the wellness experience.

Headline:
Traditional wisdom, thoughtfully experienced.

Explain Ayurveda in simple international English without promising diagnosis, cure, prevention or guaranteed results.

Include available therapies/packages only after client confirmation.

CTA:
Explore Ayurveda

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 05 — WELLNESS PHILOSOPHY / HEALING LANGUAGE
--------------------------------------------------
Potential themes:
- Body
- Mind
- Food
- Movement
- Nature
- Rest

Use "healing" as the brand's experiential/philosophical language where appropriate, but do not turn it into a medical promise.

If healing crystals are included, present them only as part of the resort's wellness philosophy/experience, not as scientifically proven medical treatment.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 06 — PROGRAMMES / PACKAGES
--------------------------------------------------
Display only confirmed structured programmes.

Package name

If price is missing:

Keep Wellness Packages distinct from Short Stay / Wellness Stay / Monthly Stay accommodation options.
