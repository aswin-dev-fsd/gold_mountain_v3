Gold Mountain Wellness Resort

Premium international-facing wellness resort website

PRIMARY GOAL:
Design and develop a premium, calm, editorial-style website for Gold Mountain Wellness Resort in Tiruvannamalai, Tamil Nadu, India.

The website must position Gold Mountain primarily as a WELLNESS RESORT.

It must NOT feel like:
- An Ayurveda clinic
- An Ayurveda treatment centre
- A retreat-only business
- A generic hotel website
- A spiritual organization
- A medical website
- A typical Indian resort booking website
- A generic wellness template

The experience should communicate:

RESORT + NATURE + WELLNESS + FOOD + LOCATION + EXPERIENCE

with wellness as the strongest supporting proposition and the resort environment as the foundation.

--------------------------------------------------
1. BRAND POSITIONING
--------------------------------------------------

Gold Mountain Wellness Resort is a peaceful wellness resort located in the natural and spiritual surroundings of Arunachala, Tiruvannamalai.

The core idea is:

"Heal the body and mind through traditional therapy and healthy food amidst nature, in the presence of divine Arunachala."

Treat this as brand/strategy language, not a guaranteed medical outcome. Do not convert it into a promise of treating, curing, preventing, or restoring a medical condition.

Guests come to Gold Mountain not only for Ayurveda or treatments, but to stay, slow down, reconnect with nature, experience the location, eat well and explore wellness at their own pace.

The resort itself is the destination.

Wellness is part of the experience.

Ayurveda is an important offering, but it should NOT dominate the entire brand.

TYPOGRAPHY RULE:
Do NOT use Garet Sans as a separate replacement family. The earlier architecture document's Garet/Aileron treatment is superseded for website implementation by the supplied brand identity direction: Outfit + The Seasons.

Tracking correction:
The brand PDF contains very aggressive "-60%" tracking notes for H1/H2. Do NOT literally implement -60% CSS letter-spacing; that would materially damage readability. Treat the notation as a design reference requiring visual tuning.

Recommended implementation starting points:
- H1: letter-spacing: -0.03em
- H2: letter-spacing: -0.025em
- H3: letter-spacing: -0.01em
- Body: letter-spacing: 0
- Accent/editorial: letter-spacing: 0 to -0.01em

Tune only after visual QA.

Font implementation:
- Prefer WOFF2 web fonts.
- Use font-display: swap.
- Preload only the most important above-the-fold font assets.
- Match fallback metrics carefully to reduce layout shift.
- Do not embed random fonts from the web.
- Confirm webfont licensing before production deployment.

11. STAY / PACKAGE TAXONOMY
--------------------------------------------------

These terms must NOT be treated as interchangeable:

SHORT STAY
Accommodation-focused visit for guests who want a flexible stay and may explore the property/local area.

WELLNESS STAY
Accommodation combined with flexible wellness experiences such as confirmed Ayurveda, yoga or meditation options.

MONTHLY STAY
Longer accommodation-focused stay with the details confirmed by the client.

WELLNESS PACKAGE
A defined, structured programme with a confirmed duration and confirmed inclusions.

Do not show a package duration, inclusion or price until confirmed.
Do not label an accommodation-only product as a wellness programme.

--------------------------------------------------
15. ANIMATION SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------

Animation is part of the visual identity, not a separate decoration layer.

PRINCIPLES:
- Slow, organic, understated
- Use motion to reveal hierarchy and atmosphere
- Prefer transform and opacity animation
- Avoid layout-triggering animation
- Avoid constant motion
- Keep essential content readable without animation

SIGNATURE INTERACTIONS:
1. Cinematic Arunachala hero reveal
2. Slow foreground/mountain depth movement
3. Healing journey: BODY → MIND → FOOD → LIFESTYLE → ENVIRONMENT
4. Living typography for key emotional statements
5. Farm-to-table visual transition
6. Choose Your Journey interactive stay/package panels
7. Subtle magnetic CTA treatment
8. Full-width property walkthrough transitions
9. Horizontal experience storytelling section
10. Day-at-Gold-Mountain timeline with changing light/visual rhythm
11. Restrained gold thread/divider motion
12. "Breathing" landscape effect for selected hero/environment imagery

Implementation preference:
GSAP + ScrollTrigger may be used for sophisticated scroll sequencing when justified. Keep the dependency footprint small and clean up triggers correctly in component lifecycles.

Accessibility:
Honor @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce). Replace non-essential movement with fades/static states. Do not hide essential information behind animation.

Performance:
- Animate transform/opacity where possible
- Avoid giant DOM-driven effect stacks
- Avoid large video backgrounds unless optimized and clearly justified
- Keep above-the-fold animation lightweight
- Do not delay meaningful content waiting for an animation
